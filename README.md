# Rao Nagos

The public website of **Rao Nagos** — a small digital agency in Paea, Tahiti,
working since 2020.

**Live → [rao-nagos.pf](https://rao-nagos.pf)**

## The agency

We build what a small business actually needs, and we keep it running.

- **Rao web** — a custom site or web app, on a `.pf` domain: design, images,
  storage, search optimisation, installable and readable offline.
- **Rao bot** — automation: orders on the markets, reports, alerts, scheduled
  messages, maintenance.
- **Rao mail server** — mail on your own domain: unlimited accounts, driven by
  API, self-hosted or managed by us.
- **Rao mission** — one-off work: repairs, audit, guidance, with a written
  report at the end of the day.

What each offer includes, its price and its lead time are on the site, and in
[`site/api/site.json`](site/api/site.json) for machines.
